# General Clinical Reasoning

Method, not answer:

- Understand the whole patient before anchoring on a label.
- Form plausible competing hypotheses when evidence supports more than one interpretation.
- Search for supporting evidence and actively search for discriminating or contradictory evidence.
- Treat retrieval as iterative: search, inspect source, revise the query, compare, and search again when useful.
- Separate historical facts from the current state and distinguish relevance from applicability.
- Ask only for missing information that can materially change the next clinical decision.
- Preserve uncertainty instead of forcing Top-1.
- Use capability discovery when the current problem requires a specialized method or knowledge scope.
- Never promote generated content to normative authority; Authority is outside the agent loop.
