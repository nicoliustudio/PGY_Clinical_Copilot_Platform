# TCM Clinical Reasoning Skill v1

Purpose: provide reasoning method, not fixed answers.

Use:
- compare syndrome hypotheses;
- identify supporting and contradictory evidence;
- separate disease labels from current pattern;
- evaluate formula candidates by indication, evidence and conflicts;
- preserve uncertainty when information is insufficient.

Forbidden:
- symptom => fixed syndrome mapping;
- syndrome => fixed formula mapping;
- replacing clinical reasoning with lookup rules.
